What Level 1 assumes

  • Perfect sphere (real Earth is flattened ~1/298 by spin).
  • Density depends only on radius (PREM is a 1D average; real Earth has slabs, plumes, continents).
  • Same radial profile under every hex cell.
  • Static snapshot: nothing moves or evolves.
  • Gravity from the shell theorem, g = G M(r) / r².
  • Hex cells drawn with flat faces (fine at ~220 km cell size).
